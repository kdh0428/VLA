### Where does the nearby 'p = 7.3e-4' come from?

- '7.3e-4' is **not** a p value specific to the +4.37 m endpoint. In EXPERIMENT_SUMMARY.md it is the Holm-adjusted p of the predeclared P1A family (m = 3) printed after the *correction* contrast P1A-3 (−3.50 m, OC_refR − OC); all three P1A contrasts sit at the 13-drive floor p = 2.4e-04 (all 13 drives same sign), so each Holm p is 3 × floor = 7.3e-04.
- In PAPER_NUMBERS.md the string 'p = 7.3e-4, Holm 9.8e-4' belongs to a different contrast again: the post-hoc model − CM endpoint excess in A− (+2.35 m), i.e. the constant-motion baseline comparison, not the Geo endpoint.
- PAPER_NUMBERS.md line 317 attaches 'predeclared family Holm 7.3e-4' to the V3 numbers [3.32, 5.19]; the predeclared Holm belongs to V2. This is a label mix, not a numerical error, but it must be fixed.
- MANUSCRIPT_REVISION.md (contribution 2) and CLAIM_EVIDENCE_MATRIX.md quote the V3 numbers ([3.32, 5.19], Holm 9.8e-4) under a 'beyond pure coordinate accumulation' description, which matches V3's semantics; they must then be labelled post-hoc, while EXPERIMENT_SUMMARY.md l.361 quotes V2 (with the 5.18 error).
