**Gripper.** Excluded from the amplification metric (dims d+1…5 only). In the environment the gripper value (mask false, raw normalised n) is binarised: a = sign(2n − 1), then inverted (`to_env_action`, as `run_libero_eval.py`). Every gripper-bin change observed under the x perturbation flipped the binarised command (rates in the table).

Reading: the bin-level 92.6% is robust to the cluster unit (initial-state CI similar to the task CI). In physical units the downstream response is real but smaller than the bin count suggests: a ±8-bin x shift (≈2.6 mm commanded) moves the downstream translation by at least as much in the 'physical amplification' share shown, and rotation dims carry most of the bin movement. This physical criterion is post-hoc and not a replacement for the original metric.
