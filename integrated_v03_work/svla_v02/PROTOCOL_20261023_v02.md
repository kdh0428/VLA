# PROTOCOL_20261023_v02 — SpatialVLA within-call follow-up with distance-matched donors (B)

- **Status.** Pre-registered before any v02 intervention output exists. Hashes and time are in `PROTOCOL_LOCK_v02.txt`.
- **Relation to v01.** v01 (2026-10-22) is the discovery stage. v02 is a follow-up with a donor rule changed in advance, so it is a separate experiment. v01 and v02 observations are never pooled. Because tasks and the move_near layouts are reused, v02 is **not** an independent task-generalization test.

## Fixed from v01 (unchanged)
- **Model and path.** Model and checkpoint: `spatialvla-4b-224-sft-fractal`. The evaluation path is the official model and processor with a **fresh `generate` cache per call** (A3 of v01). `svla_wc_core.py` is byte-identical to the v01 final run; precision and kernels are unchanged.
- **Arms and edits.** Arms N, R, O, L, OL. Actions are numbered 1–4. Emitted a1–a2 are kept natural, and the edit replaces whole 3-token actions in the conditioning copy only.
- **Response metric.** The response space is the 6 normalized motion components (decoded before unnormalization). The gripper token of the donor must equal the natural one at both positions.
  - D(X,Y) = (1/12) Σ_{t=3,4} Σ_{d=1..6} |u_X − u_Y|
  - E_L = D(L,R), E_O = D(O,R), Δ = E_L − E_O.
- **Meaning of O.** O changes a1 while a2 is forced to its natural value, so the a1 → a2 → a3–a4 path is held fixed. The measured quantity is a position-specific context response with the intermediate action fixed, not total error propagation.

## Sample (B)
- **coke.** CPU survey of seeds 4000–4099 (`manifest/survey_coke_4000_4099.json`: full actor and robot poses, coarse and full fingerprints). The panel is the first 25 layouts in seed order that are disjoint from the v01 pilot (2000–2024) and validation (3000–3011) coarse fingerprints and unique by full fingerprint: seeds 4000–4024.
- **move_near.** The same 10 layouts, representative seeds and instructions as v01. These are **reused** layouts, not new independent layouts.
- **Observations.** Images before natural generation calls 2, 6 and 10 (= control steps 2, 6, 10 under the official per-step adapter). At most 105. An episode that ends early contributes fewer observations, and none are duplicated. Input image hash, layout fingerprint and instruction are recorded, so identical inputs to v01 can be detected and disclosed.
- **Manifest.** `manifest/pilot_layouts_v02.json`.

## Donor rule (new)
1. Up to **64** official samples per observation (T = 0.7, top_p = 1.0, top_k = 0), generated in order j = 0, 1, …. The seed of sample j is sha256("svla_wc_v02_src:" + obs_id + ":" + j)[:4], read as a little-endian integer.
2. A sample is joint-valid when its a1 and a2 token types are valid, its gripper tokens equal the natural ones at both positions, and d_O > 0 and d_L > 0. Here d_O = mean_d |u_src(a1) − u_N(a1)| and d_L = mean_d |u_src(a2) − u_N(a2)|.
3. The **first joint-valid sample with 0.8 ≤ d_L/d_O ≤ 1.25** becomes the common donor for O, L and OL. Sampling stops there; no continuation has been generated at that point.
4. If no sample qualifies within 64, the observation is MATCHED_SOURCE_MISSING. There is no further seed or temperature search, no wider tolerance and no reselection.
5. Every generated sample is stored with its rejection reason, distances, ratio and selected index. The translation/rotation change pattern at a1 and at a2 is recorded as well.

## Path checks
- **Smoke.** Parity only, on `manifest/smoke_list.json`: coke seeds 3008 and 3009, plus move_near seeds 6 and 8, at call 0. Not analyzed.
- **Per observation.** Every observation checks:
  - official output equals collection-time `predict_action`;
  - adapter natural output equals official;
  - R a3–a4 raw tokens and EOS equal the natural ones;
  - decoded actions are equal.
- **On failure.** A failure gives status PARITY_FAIL: no arms are run, and effects are not interpreted for that run until the cause is separated. Failing observations are never silently dropped. Short or invalid outputs are never padded or clipped into validity.

## Analysis (`scripts/analyze_v02.py`, standalone decoder; fixed before running)
- **Primary.** Δ over all analyzed observations; every analyzed observation is distance-matched by construction.
- **Aggregation.** Average within layout, then over layouts within a task (equal weights), then over the two tasks (equal weights).
- **Interval.** Bootstrap with 10,000 resamples and seed **20261023**: layouts resampled within each task (task fixed); 95% percentile interval.
- **NA rules.** If a task has no analyzed observation, the two-task estimate is NA. If a task has ≤ 1 layout, the interval is NA.
- **Secondary analyses.**
  - Per-task results.
  - Responses at a3 and a4.
  - Translation vs rotation.
  - The subset with the same change-type pattern at a1 and a2.
  - Gripper change rates.
  - D(OL, L) computed directly.
  - J = E_OL − E_O − E_L (descriptive; not an equivalence test).
  - Per-observation and per-layout Δ distributions.
  - Donor distances.
- **Denominators.** Candidate layouts → collected observations → valid natural outputs → R pass → matched source → valid arms → analyzed. Reported per task, per layout and per observation, together with source attempts, rejection reasons and the ratio distribution.
- **Coverage target** (a design target, not a power calculation): at least 15 coke and 8 move_near matched layouts. The actual coverage is reported as it comes out, with no extra sources or observations if the target is missed.
- **Results.** All directions are reported as found. Command differences are never described as physical motion.
