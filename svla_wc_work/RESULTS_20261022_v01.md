# RESULTS_20261022_v01 — SpatialVLA within-call position intervention (pilot)

**Scope of the estimates.**
- Checkpoint: spatialvla-4b-224-sft-fractal. Tasks: Google-robot pick-coke-can and move-near in SimplerEnv. Observations: initial and early natural-rollout states.
- Response space: the 6 normalized motion components of a3–a4, before unnormalization. These are **not metres, radians or end-effector motion**; Euler-component differences are not geodesic rotation distances.
- Intervals: exploratory layout-bootstrap intervals, conditional on these two tasks.
- Source: everything below is recomputed on CPU from raw token ids (`scripts/analyze.py`), and the denominators and point estimates are re-derived by an independent implementation (`scripts/verify_cpu.py`: ALL_MATCH).

**Descriptive context** (computed after the results; post hoc):
- **Token changes.** L changed the a3–a4 tokens in 89% of analyzed observations, O in 27%. E_O was exactly 0 in 73%.
- **Donor distances.** Donors were on average farther for L (mean d_L 0.068 vs d_O 0.054; median ratio 1.43; 55% above 1.25, 34% below 0.8), so the all-observation Δ is partly confounded by donor distance.
- **Response per unit distance.** Mean E/d is 1.67 for L vs 0.67 for O.
- **Correlations.** corr(d_L, E_L) = 0.40; corr(d_O, E_O) = −0.08.

## Reading
- **Path.** The within-call intervention path is verified for this SigLIP/Gemma 2 policy. Self-patch is token-identical in 117/117 observations (12 validation + 105 pilot), with bitwise-equal continuation logits.
- **Direction.** Editing the latest preceding action (a2) changes the continuation much more often and by more than editing the older one (a1). This holds in both tasks, at both a3 and a4, and in both translation and rotation.
- **Primary analysis.** The pre-specified distance-matched estimate is positive (0.084) but rests on 7 observations. Its two-task interval is undefined, so the primary analysis does not by itself establish the effect.
- **Secondary support.** The supporting evidence is the all-joint-valid secondary analysis, 0.070 [0.044, 0.100], which is partly confounded by donor distance.
- **Older edit.** Adding the older edit to the latest one still changes the continuation (D(OL,L) = 0.043), so the older position is not irrelevant. J near zero is not evidence of additivity.
- **Not shown.** Physical motion, task success, generalization beyond these two tasks, and generalization to other backbones.
