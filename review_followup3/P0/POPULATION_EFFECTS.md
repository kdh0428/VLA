## 1. Selection flow (P0-A.1)

- Dev natural pass = `outputs/full_extract/records.jsonl`, arm N (navtest shards 0–5): 2,748 records; 1 CoT-stub scene removed -> **2,747 non-CoT natural scenes** (28 logs / 20 drives / 8 days / 12 vehicles / 14 day–vehicles). No runaway / short plans.
- A− / A+ = the dev action-category rule (`analyze_action_history.a_eval` -> `pra_labels.label_A`: longitudinal and lateral class of the 10-pose plan accepted vs GT). **A− = 52** (13 drives / 10 day–vehicles), A+ = 2,695. Severe (A− and FDE5 > 3 m, the 'amplification' outcome applied to the natural plan) = 40; non-severe A− = 12.
- Selection eligibility needs a first token mismatch with GT within 10 tokens: 1,159 scenes; 52 of them A−; **A− scenes without a mismatch = 0**. So the 52 selected A− are *all* A− scenes of the 2,747 under this rule (verified, not assumed).
- A+ selection (exact replica of `scripts/equal_distance_perturbation.py`): for every A− at first-mismatch step t*, 3 A+ scenes with the same t*, drawn from the token-sorted pool after `random.Random(0).shuffle`. Replica = stored run: 208 selected, set identical = True, group labels agree 208/208, t* agree 208/208.

## 4. Is the population effect a stratum mixture? (P0-A.5)

Checked before decomposing: same frame (E), same scene-weighted Hajek estimand, known π, weights summing to the frame size. All hold for P1-C, so θ_P = (W_F θ_F + W_S θ_S)/(W_F + W_S) exactly:

| contrast | W_F | W_S | F share of frame | θ_F | θ_S (Hajek) | θ_P | F contribution | S contribution |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| RN | 105 | 2086 | 4.8% | -34.2 | -2.6 | -4.1 | -1.64 | -2.49 |
| RV | 105 | 2086 | 4.8% | +26.8 | +2.5 | +3.6 | +1.29 | +2.36 |
| DM | 105 | 2086 | 4.8% | +6.2 | +2.4 | +2.6 | +0.30 | +2.32 |

For RN, 60% of the population effect comes from the A+ (S) stratum. The dev mechanism set has no valid decomposition (A+ sampled only at t* values present among A−; π = 0 elsewhere), and no prevalence × effect product is formed anywhere in P0.
