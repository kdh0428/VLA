The kit is retained under `../format/`. The 2027 style removes the visible paper ID; its files were not manually adjusted. Earlier 2026 format notes are archived under `../history_v03/`.

Before submission, the authors must confirm that other tools used throughout the research are also named and that the stated scope is complete. The draft is not a certification of author enrollment or administrative submission status.

Overleaf: upload the contents of this directory with its subdirectories; use `main.tex` and pdfLaTeX. Build `supplementary.tex` separately for the supplement. For the checked local build, run `python3 build_20261023_v04.py` from the package root.
